# Ideas

Not built. Just things CAVEN could do later.

The main idea: *user says "Start Morning" then it continues in morning routine -> CAVEN : "I hope you had a good sleep sir, Did you have anything planned for today, Great why dot you drink some water"** user says done. Then the next step.

He never makes up your routine. You tell him the steps once. He saves them only when you say save.

---

## Routines he could run

- **Morning** — bed to out the door
- **Work start** — name the one job, start it
- **Shut the day down** — what’s left, what goes to tomorrow
- **Bedtime** — lights, charger, phone down, tomorrow’s first thing
- **Heading out** — keys, wallet, phone, lights (20 seconds)
- **Come home** — bag down, what’s tonight
- **Sunday** — money, leftover tasks, week ahead
- **After a bad day** — have you eaten, what’s left, what can wait

If you’re late: short version. If you mumble: “Sorry?” Same step. Don’t start over.

---

## Same idea, other jobs

- **What am I doing first?** — he names one task, not a list
- **Stay with me for 20 minutes** — checks in once, then asks if it’s done
- **Talk me through this** — kitchen, packing, a simple workout you already described
- **Where was I?** — last unfinished step after you got interrupted
- **Read me the day** — next event, reminders, one habit. Then stop
- **Reminder that starts a drill** — “gym bag” becomes bag / shoes / bottle / out

---

## Other help (not a routine)

- Remind you of a note when it matters (“passport’s in the blue drawer” — only if you said so)
- “Can I spend this?” from your real budget
- “Have I got room Thursday?” yes or no
- You talk, he splits it into tasks/notes and checks before saving
- “Be straight with me” — one honest pushback
- Practice a hard conversation — play it back shorter
- Shop list / packing list from what you actually said
- Sit with you while you wait (train, doctor). Just talk. No cards

---

## Don’t do these

- Invent a “perfect morning”
- Cheerleading or badges
- Long speeches
- Open a card just because you said “morning”
- Pretend something is saved when it isn’t

---

“Skip that.” / “Short version.” / “Where was I?”
